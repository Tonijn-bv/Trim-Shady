Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens with your personal data when you visit this page or sign up for news about Trim Shady. In short: we only ask for your email address, we use it only to tell you about Trim Shady, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Who we are

Trim Shady is made by Tonijn BV, Jan Van Beersstraat 4, 2018 Antwerpen, Belgium, VAT BE 0872 230 829. Tonijn BV is responsible (the "controller") for the personal data described here.

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@tonijn.net.

What we collect, and why

When you sign up

We collect your email address, together with the date and time of your sign-up and of your confirmation. After signing up you get the Trim Shady download right away. We use your address to send you occasional news and updates about Trim Shady. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give it by signing up and confirming through the link in our first email (double opt-in); without that confirmation we do not send you news. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to us.

When you visit the page

Like every website, the server that hosts this page receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. This is needed to deliver the page and to keep it secure (our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to identify you, and this page does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Services we use

We work with a few trusted providers. They process data on our behalf and only as needed for their service:

  • Kit (ConvertKit LLC, United States) stores the email list and sends our emails. The sign-up form on this page sends your address directly to Kit.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts this page and receives the technical visit data described above.
  • Vimeo (Vimeo.com, Inc., United States) plays the intro film. We embed it with Vimeo's "do not track" setting, so the player does not track your viewing behavior with cookies or analytics.

Because these providers are based in the United States, your data may be transferred outside the European Union. Such transfers are protected by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

The fonts on this page are served from our own site, not from an external font service.

Cookies

This page does not set cookies of its own. Kit's form script may store a small technical value in your browser so the form works correctly; it is not used for advertising.

How long we keep your data

We keep your email address for as long as you stay subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we stop sending emails right away and remove your address from our list within 30 days, unless we must keep a record of your unsubscribe to make sure you are not emailed again. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are removed after 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to privacy@tonijn.net and we will reply within one month.

If you feel we have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

Changes

When this notice changes, we update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will let subscribers know by email.
